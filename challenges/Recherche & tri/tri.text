3 - tri par selection / selection sorting

nafs method ta3 sorting by insertion , ghir howa hna kanamchiw n9albo 3la lower number

[29 , 10 , 14 , 37 , 13] = 10 is lower number (compari m3a number 1 fi array)
[10 | 29 , 14 , 37 , 13] = 13 is lower number (fi jiha li mazal mam9ada)
[10 , 13 | 14 , 37 , 29] = 13 tbadal howa wo 29 , wo 13 dkhal fi jiha li m9ada 
[10 , 13 , 14 | 37 , 29] 
[10 , 13 , 14 , 29 , 37]
